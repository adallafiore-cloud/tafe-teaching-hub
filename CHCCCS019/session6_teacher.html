Marking Notes

Common Issues to Watch For in Written Tasks

AT2b — Denise written questions
  • Students must identify the trauma response as a crisis (PTSD trigger from car backfire) — not just 'Denise was upset'
  • Communication techniques must include specific examples — not just 'I used active listening'
  • Duty of care answer must include all three: self, Denise, other clients in reception
  • Paperwork: Incident Record form + notify supervisor = the minimum expected
AT3b — Serena written questions
  • Safety assessment must address risk to Serena specifically — not generic crisis response
  • Students should identify DFV history as context for Serena's distress
  • Legal/ethical considerations: duty of care, privacy/confidentiality in DFV context, personal safety intervention order
AT4b — Colman documentation
  • Incident Record must be completed in full — accurate, objective, no assumptions or judgements
  • Crisis Intervention Plan should reflect a collaborative planning process — not a plan imposed on Colman
  • Written questions: students must demonstrate integration of legal/ethical framework with practical response
💡
Documentation quality: Remind students that documentation in community services must be factual, accurate, and objective. No assumptions, no judgements, no speculation. If it wasn't observed or said directly, it doesn't go in the record.
