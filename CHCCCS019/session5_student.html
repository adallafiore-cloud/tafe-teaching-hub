Role Play Summaries

What to Expect Today

AT2 — Role Play

Recognise and Respond to Crisis — Denise

You are a client services officer at First Access. You have just finished assisting Denise with Centrelink and her car registration. You are saying goodbye in the busy reception area when a car backfires outside — Denise has a severe trauma response linked to her history of being shot by her partner.

Your assessor will look for:

  • Identifying signs of crisis and safety risks
  • Appropriate communication — active listening, affirming safety and agency
  • Asking questions to assess risk
  • Balancing collaboration and direction
  • Following duty of care procedures
AT3 — Role Play

Recognise and Respond to Crisis — Serena

Serena has experienced domestic violence and has a personal safety intervention order against her ex-partner. She arrives at First Access an hour early, extremely distressed and agitated. You are called out to receive her — there is no one else in the waiting room.

AT4 — Role Play (Two Parts)

Recognise and Respond to Crisis — Colman

Part A: Respond to Colman's initial crisis.
Part B: Follow up with Colman.

Full scenario and instructions are in your Student Assessment Booklet. Make sure you have read and understood both parts.

What the Assessor is Looking For

Assessment Criteria — All Role Plays

  • Identify that the client is experiencing a crisis or trauma response
  • Monitor for signs that the client presents a safety risk to themselves or others
  • Take action in response to safety risk indicators (asking questions, moving others, calling for help)
  • Use active listening skills — open body language, eye contact, attentive listening, reflection
  • Affirm the client's capacity, agency, and sense of safety
  • Balance collaboration and direction based on the client's capacity to make decisions
  • Assist the client in making a decision about a next step
  • Demonstrate awareness of legal and ethical responsibilities (duty of care)
