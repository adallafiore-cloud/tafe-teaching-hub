AT2 — Denise

Role Play Instructions and Marking Notes

Setting up the role play

AT2 — Denise (PTSD/Trauma Response)

Setup: Student stands at reception desk. Assessor plays Denise. 1–2 people seated in waiting area (use other staff or students not being assessed).

Trigger: Play car backfire audio when the student is mid-farewell. One person in waiting room says loudly: "Don't worry, it was just a car backfiring." Denise does not hear/register this.

Denise's behaviour: Jerk and shake. Clench/unclench fists. Cry. Say "I just can't go on." "It's all too much." "I just want to die." Stare towards a window. Do NOT respond to student unless they make an affirming statement. If student affirms capacity → become receptive. If student offers accommodation → accept and appear reassured. If student offers counselling → accept with relief.

Verbal questions after:

  • Whose safety were you responsible for? (Answer: yourself, Denise, other clients)
  • Two physical indicators Denise presented a safety risk? (Any two: clenching fists, shaking, jerking, non-responsive/staring, seeming afraid)
  • What next steps for paperwork? (Incident record, notify supervisor)
AT3 — Serena

Role Play Instructions

Setting up the role play

AT3 — Serena (DFV/Panic Response)

Serena arrives an hour early, extremely distressed and agitated. Student is called out to receive her. No one else in waiting room.

Full role play instructions are in the CHCCCS019 Marking Guide assessor documents. As assessor, play Serena — display acute anxiety, pacing, difficulty communicating. Gradually respond to student's calming techniques.

Observe for: crisis identification, communication techniques, safety assessment, affirming safety, referral/next steps.

AT4 — Colman

Role Play Instructions

Two-part role play

AT4 — Colman (Parts A and B)

Part A: Respond to Colman's initial crisis. Full scenario and assessor instructions in the Marking Guide.

Part B: Follow-up with Colman. Student should demonstrate ability to continue supportive practice, check on safety, and move towards referral/documentation.

Documentation required for AT4b (written task in Session 6): Incident Record + Crisis Intervention Plan.
